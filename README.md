## 🌹 A Modern Alternative to Cardboard Cards

Every February 14th, millions of couples exchange generic supermarket cards that end up in drawers. **[Heartreveals](https://heartreveals.com)** provides a thoughtful, eco-friendly, and interactive alternative:

- 💌 **Animated Envelope with Seal**: A personalized letter styled in calligraphy that opens smoothly on screen.
- ⏳ **Midnight Countdown**: Build anticipation by having your surprise unlock automatically at midnight on Valentine's Day.
- 🖼️ **Couples Memory Carousel**: Up to 8 cherished photos celebrating your moments together.
- 🎟️ **Digital Scratch-Offs**: Fun interactive coupons (*"One romantic candlelit dinner"*, *"A massage"*, *"A surprise getaway"*).
- 🎶 **Curated Romantic Soundtracks**: Soft acoustic and piano themes to accompany the reading.
- 📱 **Instant Private Delivery**: Send a secret link over WhatsApp / iMessage or print a custom QR code on a card or ribbon.
